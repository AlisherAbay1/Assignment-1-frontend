Delivery

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available at checkout for an extra fee. You'll get a tracking link by email once your order ships.

Payment

We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, and bank transfer. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

Support

Reach us via the contact form, email, or live chat. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days (Mon–Fri).
